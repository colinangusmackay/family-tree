> 1880 DEATHS in the Parish of Douglas in the County of Lanark
>
> <br/>
>
> No. 27
>
> Name and Surname: Alexander Gilchrist
>
> Occupation: Agricultural Labourer
>
> Condition: Widow of Elizabeth Irvine
>
> When and Where Died: 1880 August Fifteenth 5H A.M.; Douglas
>
> Sex: M
>
> Age: 80 years
>

>
