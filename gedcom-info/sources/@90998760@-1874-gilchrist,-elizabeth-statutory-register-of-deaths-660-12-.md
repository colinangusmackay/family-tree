## Text

> 1874, DEATHS in the Parish of Wiston and Roberton in the County of Lanark
>
> <br/>
>
> See Register of Corrected Entries Vol. 1 Page 9th dated December 16th 1874
>
> Nol 12
>
> Name and Surname: Elizabeth Gilchrist
>
> Condition: Married to Alexander Gilchrist Pauper, Formerly Labourer
>
> When and Where Died: 1874 December Second 3H 0M P.M.; Roberton
>
> Sex: F
>
> Age: 73 years
>
> <br/>
>
> Name, Surname and Profession of Father: John Irvine; Hand Loom Weaver (deceased)
>
> Name and Maiden Surname of Mother: Helen Irvine M.S. Connel (deceased)
>
> <br/>
>
> Cause of death: Not certified; Dropped down and died
>
> Name and Qualification of Informant: Alex. Gilchrist; Widower (present)
>
> When and Where Registered: 1874 December 3rd at Wiston
>
> Registrar: William Core
>
> <br/>
>
> ---
>
> <br/>
>
> To the Registrar of the Parish of Wiston & Roberton.
>
> Result of Precognition touching the Death of the
>
> undermentioned Person (17 & 18 Vic. Cap. 80 Sect. 40)
>
> Registered under No. 12, 1874.
>
> Name: Elizabeth Irvine or Gilchrist
>
> Age and Sex: 73 years, Female
>
> When died: 2nd December 1874
>
> Where died: In the house of her husband Alexander Gilchrist, Labourer, residing at Roberton in the parish of Wiston & Roberton and County of Lanark
>
> Cause of death; how long disease continued; Medical man by whom certified and when he last saw deceased: Heart disease and Bronchitis of long duration. Certified by James Dickson, Surgeon, Roberton, who made post-mortem examination of the body of deceased and had attended her previous to her death
>
> Burial Place and Undertaker by whom certified: Wiston Churchyard
>
> Signed: T. M. Shirley, Procurator-Fiscal
>
> Procurator Fiscal's office, Lanark 11th Dec. 1874
>
> At Wiston, William Core, Registrar — December 14th 1874
>
