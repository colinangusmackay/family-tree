## Text

> 1909 DEATHS in the Parish of Dornoch in the County of Sutherland
>
> <br/>
>
> No. 33
>

>
> Name and Surname: Mary Ann Ross
>
> Condition: Widow of Alexander Ross, Fisherman
>
> <br/>
>

>
> Sex: F
>
> Age: 90 years
>

>

>
