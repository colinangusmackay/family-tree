## Text

> 1940 DEATHS in the District of Dornoch in the County of Sutherland
>
> <br/>
>
> No. 18
>
> Name and Surname: Annie Cumming
>
> Condition: Widow of Adam Cumming, Fisherman
>
> When and Where Died: 1940 April TwentyFirst 10H 15M P.M. at Boston House, Embo
>
> Sex: F
>
> Age: 82 years
>
> <br/>
>
> Name, Surname and Profession of Father: George Mackay, Fisherman (deceased)
>
> Name, and maiden Surname of Mother; Lucy Mackay M.S. Matheson (deceased)
>
> <br/>
>
> Cause of Death: Bronchitis - 2 months; Intestinal Stasus; Myocardia degeneration; Coronary Thrombosis
>
> Medical Attendant by whom certified: Henry Mackay, M.A. M.B. Ch.B
>
> <br/>
>
> Signature and qualification of Informant: Wm. A. Mackay (nephew)
>
> When and Where registered: 1940 April 22nd at Dornoch
>
> Registrar: W. Skinner
>
