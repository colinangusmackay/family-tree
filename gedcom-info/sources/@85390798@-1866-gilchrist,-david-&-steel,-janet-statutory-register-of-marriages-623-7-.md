## Text

> 1866, MARRIAGES in the Parish of Biggar in the County of Lanark
>
> <br/>
>
> No. 7
>
> When, Where and How Married: 1866 On the Eighth day of June, at Biggar; After Banns according to the fors of the United Presbyterian Church
>
> <br/>
>
> GROOM:
>
> Signature: David Gilchrist
>
> Profession: Ploughman
>
> Condition: Bachelor
>
> Age: 23
>
> Usual Residence: Skirling
>
> Name, Surname and Profession of Father: Alexander Gilchrist, Labourer
>
> Name and Maiden Surname of Mother: Elizabeth Gilchrist M.S. Irvine
>
> <br/>
>
> BRIDE:
>
> Signature: Janet Steel
>
> Profession: Farm Servant
>
> Condition: Spinster
>
> Age: 26
>
> Usual Residence: Biggar
>
> Name, Surname and Profession of Father: Mungo Steel, Blacksmith
>
> Name and Maiden Surname of Mother: Mary Steel M.S. Mc____
>
> <br/>
>
> Officiating Minister: David Smith Minister of the North U.P. Church, Biggar
>
> Witness: James Gilchrist
>
> Witness: James Brown
>
> <br/>
>
> When and Where Registered: 1866 June 9th at Biggar
>
> Registrar: D. Lockhart
>
